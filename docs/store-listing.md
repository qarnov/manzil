## Full description (4000 char limit)

Manzil is a calm, ad-free companion for daily worship. No account, no tracking,
no clutter — just the things you reach for every day.

PRAYER TIMES
Accurate prayer times calculated for your location, with a live countdown to the
next prayer so you always know how long you have. Set your own iqamah offsets
per prayer, or follow the times published by your local masjid.

QURAN
Read the full Quran in Arabic with translation and tafsir. Listen to per-ayah
recitation, mark your favourite ayat, and pick up where you left off. An ayah of
the day appears on the home screen each morning.

DUAS
Forty-six authentic duas across eight categories — morning and evening, travel,
distress, forgiveness and more — each with Arabic, translation, and the benefit
of reciting it. Search across all of them instantly.

QIBLA COMPASS
A live compass using your device's real heading and GPS, with a gentle haptic
nudge when you are aligned towards the Kaaba.

TASBIH
A digital tasbih ring for your dhikr, with SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu
Akbar built in, plus any custom dhikr you want to count. Your count is saved, and
a completed round rests at 100 so you can see it.

ZAKAT CALCULATOR
Work out your zakat simply — assets minus liabilities, checked against the nisab,
at 2.5%.

HIJRI CALENDAR
The Islamic month at a glance, with today highlighted and important observances
marked.

MASJIDS
Find masjids near you, see their published prayer times, and register your own.

LANGUAGES
Available in English, Kannada and Malayalam.

PRIVACY
Manzil has no account system, no analytics and no advertising. Your location is
used on your device to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction, and is
never sent anywhere. Your preferences stay on your phone.
