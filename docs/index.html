Privacy Policy for Manzil

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Manzil (“the app”) is a prayer times, Quran and dua companion app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Manzil does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app has no server of its own.

Location

Manzil asks for location permission so it can:

  • calculate prayer times for where you actually are, and
  • point the qibla compass towards the Kaaba.

Your location is used only on your device, in the moment, to perform these calculations. It is never transmitted to us, never stored on any server, and never shared with anyone. Prayer time and qibla calculations happen entirely offline on your phone.

You can refuse or revoke location permission in your device settings. The app will continue to work; you will need to select a location or masjid manually, and the qibla compass will be unavailable.

Data stored on your device

Manzil saves your preferences locally on your phone so the app remembers them between uses. This includes your chosen language, your selected masjid, your prayer time adjustments, your favourite ayat, and your tasbih count.

This information stays on your device. It is not backed up to us and is not readable by us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Internet connections

To show Quran text, translations, tafsir and recitation audio, the app requests that content from two public Quran APIs:

  • api.alquran.cloud
  • api.quran.com

These requests ask only for Quran content, such as a surah number. They do not include your location, your identity, or any personal information. As with any internet request, the operators of those services will see your device’s IP address; their own privacy practices govern that, and we have no relationship with them beyond requesting public Quran data.

Children

Manzil contains no content unsuitable for children, collects nothing, and shows no advertising.
